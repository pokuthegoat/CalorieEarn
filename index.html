How it works

One simple loop, every day.

  1. 1

    Log

    Search packaged foods, scan a barcode, take a photo or enter a food manually.

  2. 2

    Review

    Check the portions and calories, and tap any food to adjust it before saving.

  3. 3

    Check in

    Log or repeat a meal, then check in. Any 5 days a week counts, and a fresh week starts Monday.

  4. 4

    Earn

    Earn $CalorieEarn under the campaign’s published rules.

Rewards

Rewards recognise participation.

You’re rewarded for consistent logging and daily check-ins, under each campaign’s published rules.

Rewarded
  • Consistent logging
  • Daily check-ins
Not rewarded
  • Eating less
  • Losing weight
  • Hitting an exact calorie number
  • No purchase, deposit or trading required
  • No wallet needed to start
  • Your diary, check-ins and progress work fully without rewards
  • Campaigns need an account. Logging doesn’t.
  • A rewards screen shows what you’ve earned and whether a campaign is running
  • Each campaign publishes its daily amount, weekly bonus, dates and eligibility first
  • One award per day. What or how much you eat never changes it
  • Photos never count toward rewards

$CalorieEarn rewards are not guaranteed cash earnings, and their value can change.

Privacy

Your food records stay private.

Private on your deviceYour diary is stored in the app’s private storage on your device, and the app has no analytics or crash reporting.
Optional accountSign in with Apple to back up your diary privately and sync it across devices. Only your Apple account’s private ID is used. No name or email is collected.
Your data, your callExport your diary as a JSON file, or delete all of it from your device.
Younger usersUnder 18 gets a simple food journal: no calorie numbers, no targets or diet plans, and no reward campaigns.
