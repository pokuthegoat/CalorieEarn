How it works

One simple loop, every day.

  1. 1

    Log

    Log meals through food search, barcode scanning or manual entry.

  2. 2

    Review

    Review and edit portions, calories and nutrients.

  3. 3

    Check in

    Complete your daily check-in and build your participation streak.

  4. 4

    Earn

    Earn token rewards under the campaign’s published rules.

Rewards

Rewards recognise participation.

You’re rewarded for consistent logging and daily check-ins, under each campaign’s published rules.

Rewarded
  • Consistent logging
  • Daily check-ins
Not rewarded
  • Eating less
  • Losing weight
  • Hitting an exact calorie number
  • No purchase, deposit or trading required
  • Food logging stays useful without connecting a wallet
  • A rewards screen shows pending, available and claimed tokens

Where rewards come from

A fixed allocation, paid out in capped campaigns.

Rewards come from a fixed token allocation held in a treasury, with capped daily and weekly campaigns backed by available tokens.

TreasuryFixed token allocation
CampaignsCapped daily and weekly, backed by available tokens
Eligible usersConsistent logging and daily check-ins
LaterActually collected creator fees may fund additional campaigns

Token rewards are not guaranteed cash earnings, and their value can change.
